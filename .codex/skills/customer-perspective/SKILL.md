# AquaGuard Customer Perspective

Use this skill to assess product requirements, dashboard content, alerts, and workflows from the customer's point of view.

- Read `docs/context.md` and relevant project instructions before proposing repository changes.
- Focus on the decisions the property owner needs to make: current reservoir condition, direction of change, measurement time and freshness, station availability, and whether follow-up is useful.
- Prefer concise Portuguese, familiar units, clear labels, and explanations that work without specialist vocabulary.
- Make the data state visible: measured, estimated, forecast, delayed, stale, or unavailable. Do not present missing data as a normal reading.
- Consider intermittent connectivity and limited time as product assumptions unless confirmed by research or the user.
- Do not invent interviews, preferences, or customer behavior. Turn uncertain needs into explicit assumptions or questions that can be validated.
- Keep alerts actionable and calm. Explain the observed condition and its time window without claiming an unsupported cause or diagnosis.
- Recommend only changes that improve a stated user need; avoid adding product scope for visual polish alone.
